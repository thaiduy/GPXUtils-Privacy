Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

GPX Utils has no account and collects no personal data for itself. Your files, your location, and your Health data stay on your devices and in your iCloud Drive. The one exception is Live Share: while you share, your location and the track you follow are sent to the app's server so that people with the link can see them.

What GPX Utils Does

GPX Utils opens GPX files on iPhone, iPad, and Mac. It shows tracks and waypoints on a map, shows elevation profiles, lets you add and edit waypoints, and lets you follow a track with on-track and off-track status. It can import a route from a Google Maps link and, on iPhone and iPad, workouts from Apple Health, and it can share your progress with a link while you follow a track. All file reading, editing, and track calculations happen on your device.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • No location data leaves your device, except while you use Live Share
  • No GPX files are uploaded; Live Share sends only the track you follow
  • No Health data is sent to us or to anyone else
  • No name, e-mail address, or other contact information is collected
  • No analytics or crash data are sent to us
  • No account or sign-in is required
  • No advertising identifiers are used

Location Access

GPX Utils asks for location access only when you tap the location button or turn on Follow Track. Your location is used on your device to show your position on the map, to place new documents near you, and to compare your position with the selected track. While Follow Track is on, iPhone and iPad keep receiving location updates with the screen off; iOS shows the location indicator during that time. Location updates stop when you turn Follow Track off. GPX Utils does not write your location to your files. Your location leaves your device only while you use Live Share (see below).

Apple Health

On iPhone and iPad, Import Workout from Health reads your workouts and their routes from Apple Health. It reads them only after you allow access, and only while the Import Workout screen is open. GPX Utils reads the list of workouts (activity type, date, duration, distance, and the name of the app that recorded each one). For the workouts you add, it also reads the route points (latitude, longitude, altitude, and time) and the times the workout was paused. GPX Utils writes nothing to Health.

When you tap Add, each workout's route is written into the GPX document you have open, as a track with a position, elevation, and time for each point. No heart rate or other health measurements are written. The document is saved where you keep it: on your device or in your iCloud Drive.

GPX Utils does not send Health data to us or to anyone else, and does not use it for advertising or marketing. You can turn off access in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › GPX Utils.

Your Files

GPX Utils opens only the files you choose. It reads and saves them in place, on your device or in your iCloud Drive. iCloud Drive storage is provided by Apple and is covered by Apple's privacy policy. GPX Utils does not upload your files to any other server.

Maps

GPX Utils uses Apple's MapKit to display maps. To load map images, your device requests them from Apple. These requests are handled under Apple's Privacy Policy. Apart from Import Route and Live Share, described below, GPX Utils does not send your tracks, waypoints, or location to Apple or to anyone else.

Import Route from Google Maps

When you paste a Google Maps directions link, GPX Utils reads it on your device. A short link (maps.app.goo.gl) is opened with one request to Google to get the full link; Google's privacy policy applies to that request. To find stops that the link names without coordinates, and to calculate the route, GPX Utils sends the stops' names or coordinates to Apple Maps. The resulting track is saved only in your document.

Live Share (iPhone and iPad)

Live Share is optional and off until you start it. While you share, the app sends your precise location and related data to the app's server so that people you give the link to can follow you on a web page.

What is sent. When you start a share: the name of the track you follow, its route (points with elevation), and the waypoints on it. While you share, about once a minute, and at once when your status changes: your location and the path you walked (latitude, longitude, elevation, accuracy, and time of each point), your status on the track (on track, off track, or locating, with the distance to the next waypoint or back to the track), your distance along the track, your pace, and your battery level. Your location is sent whatever its accuracy.

Who can see it. Anyone who has the link. Each share has its own link and a random identifier. The app has no account, and the data carries no name, e-mail address, or device identifier.

How long it is kept. The server deletes the shared data when you tap Stop Sharing or Stop Follow Track. If the app cannot reach the server then, it tries again while it runs. If the app is closed or the phone loses power while you share, the server deletes the data 48 hours after the last update. The server's free hosting plan has daily limits; if they are used up on the day a deletion is due, the data is deleted by the next daily cleanup, which runs at 00:30 UTC, so within about one more day.

Where. The server runs on Cloudflare. The app does not keep request logs. Cloudflare processes IP addresses to deliver requests and to apply rate limits. A person who opens the link views the map from Apple Maps (MapKit JS); Apple's privacy terms apply to that map.

Not used for. Advertising, analytics, or tracking across other apps and websites. The data is not sold or shared with anyone but the people who have the link.

Your control. Stop Sharing or Stop Follow Track at any time. Without Live Share, your location stays on your device.

Live Activities

On iPhone, Follow Track can show its status on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. The app updates the Live Activity on your device. It does not use a server or push notifications to do this.

Third-Party Services

GPX Utils includes no third-party SDKs, analytics, or advertising. It uses Apple services (MapKit, Apple Health, iCloud Drive, and In-App Purchase), one request to Google to open a short link in Import Route, and its own Live Share server, which runs on Cloudflare.

Children's Privacy

GPX Utils is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
