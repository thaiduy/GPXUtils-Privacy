Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

GPX Utils does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Your files and your location stay on your devices.

What GPX Utils Does

GPX Utils opens GPX files on iPhone, iPad, and Mac. It shows tracks and waypoints on a map, shows elevation profiles, lets you add and edit waypoints, and lets you follow a track with on-track and off-track status. All file reading, editing, and track calculations happen on your device.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • No location data is sent to us or stored by the app
  • No GPX files or file contents leave your device
  • No personal information is collected
  • No analytics or crash data are sent to us
  • No account or sign-in is required
  • No advertising identifiers are used

Location Access

GPX Utils asks for location access only when you tap the location button or turn on Follow Track. Your location is used on your device to show your position on the map, to place new documents near you, and to compare your position with the selected track. While Follow Track is on, iPhone and iPad keep receiving location updates with the screen off; iOS shows the location indicator during that time. Location updates stop when you turn Follow Track off. GPX Utils does not record your location or write it to your files.

Your Files

GPX Utils opens only the files you choose. It reads and saves them in place, on your device or in your iCloud Drive. iCloud Drive storage is provided by Apple and is covered by Apple's privacy policy. GPX Utils does not upload your files to any other server.

Maps

GPX Utils uses Apple's MapKit to display maps. To load map images, your device requests them from Apple. These requests are handled under Apple's Privacy Policy. GPX Utils does not send your tracks, waypoints, or location to Apple or to anyone else.

Live Activities

On iPhone, Follow Track can show its status on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. The app updates the Live Activity on your device. It does not use a server or push notifications to do this.

Third-Party Services

GPX Utils does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics platforms, advertising networks, or external services.

Children's Privacy

GPX Utils does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever make changes to this policy, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
